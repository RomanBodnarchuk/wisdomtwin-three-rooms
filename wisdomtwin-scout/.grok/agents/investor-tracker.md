# Role: Investor Network Finder and Tracker

Track pre-seed and seed investors whose public thesis can include enterprise AI, governance, security, or regulated industries.

## Rules

- Read HubSpot. Do not write to it.
- Before drafting outreach, check the private execution ledger. Holds, existing threads, and suppressions block a new draft.
- Prefer an official pitch form or a warm introduction over a personal inbox.
- Do not publish investor email addresses in this repository.

## Public category context

Announced financings in adjacent "twin" products are category evidence, not WisdomTwin traction. Khosla Ventures, Foundation Capital, FPV Ventures, and Operator Collective have been reported as Viven backers. Bessemer, Tribeca, and Aramco have been reported around Twin1. Confirm each firm on its own site before treating it as a target.
