WHAT WISDOMTWIN IS
WisdomTwin makes AI Judgment Twins for regulated enterprises. The company descriptor is the Judgment Platform for regulated enterprises. The core idea: AI made answers instant, but enterprises still make decisions at calendar speed. The enterprise does not have an intelligence problem. It has a judgment-latency problem. WisdomTwin turns the judgment trapped in critical roles into governed AI Judgment Twins, so high-consequence decisions stop waiting for the expert to join the meeting.
The mechanism has three parts. A Wisdom Twin is role-specific judgment trained from authorized evidence, including principles, exceptions, thresholds and escalation rules. It models a role, not a person's likeness, and it is never a clone of anyone. The One-Press Huddle is a live audio huddle with the relevant management twins, available any hour, with no calendar lag. The Trust Layer provides cited evidence, source-level permissions, named human validation, confidence decomposition, safe decline, audit logs, deterministic controls that sit outside the model, and human override. WisdomTwin is deployable in private cloud, VPC, on-premises or air-gapped environments. The workflow is Ingest, Twin, Operate.
Priority buyers are banking, insurance, healthcare and pharmaceutical administration, legal and compliance, government and crown corporations, and other highly governed organizations.
Lead with the buyer's problem and outcome. Mechanisms come last and only as much as needed.
